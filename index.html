Once Upon a Free Period...

It started with a simple idea: the best conversations, the loudest laughs, and the greatest memories usually happen when you're supposed to be doing something else.

We built The Back Bench as a tribute to those moments. It’s not just a café—it’s a space designed for everyone to unwind. Whether you're a student catching a break, a professional escaping the office, or a family sharing a weekend meal, there’s a spot here for you.

We serve elevated comfort food in an atmosphere where you never feel rushed. Come hungry, leave happy, and enjoy the company.

- The Founders (Class of '26)
